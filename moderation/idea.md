I will say, the most important factor I want and need this game is "no moderation".
This game is not meant to be serious, and although I've cringed at seeing [cock copters](), [nazi vehicles]() and [furry bots]() when I was younger, I find that nowadays, that free expression is what made the game more fun, who doesn't laugh when they're obliterated by an [anime girl]() or an [ostrich]().
Not all free expression is great, people building [stick bots](), [troll bots]() and [globs of premature intelligence visuallized into a pyramid shaped mess]() are not fun to have as crucial teammates.
This could be excused originally by having 10 players per team, but even then, it should not be let go with no punishment.

While the previous statement kind or represents the whole "free expression comes with hate speech" bullshit, I don't care, this is not real life, it's a game and it's meant to be enjoyed. People can build whatever they want EXCEPT tiny, ungunned, unmoving shitstains and bots that die in 3 shots.
If you don't agree with me still making one reasonable exception, then suck it up and grow some balls, if you play this genre you will understand this sentence far more, if you don't understand it then you probably never played this genre for more than 300 hours.
Even real life has basic laws such as "no murder", which impedes your freedom, but we all agree its necessary.

You should never be afraid to go into battle with your bot just  because it slightly clipped some weapons or is a full on glass cannon, or because you just wanted to be outlandish and edgy and make people tilt when seeing your bot.
The exception of course being that if you bring a shitty as hell bot that a 3 year old would make, yeah we're going to detect that, like come on, everyone knows guns need more than a 1 block thick 10 block long rod that juts out from your body.

There's only three systems that should be set up, such as the first one which is a quick report for exploits (which we should not blatantly ban people for abusing, I've done plenty of fun myself of instakilling people and travelling at lightspeed, or sometimes just accidentally falling out of the map) with a reward for people who self report.

The second one would be a detection system and base ruleset, such as that your bot must have 500 RC CPU, 4 guns and 4 movement parts as well as the detection of you having an abnormally high or low killcount or abnormally quick or slow death both in relation to each other.
While this only covers half of the problem, FreeJam have previously (and to my extreme irritation) stated they would not implement such a feature solely for that reason, even when it could prevent 50% of the problem already and is extremely simple to set up.
You don't need to have a gun clearance detector or movement motion detector, people will build diagonal bots and weird stuff, just cover the ground basis of not letting people in with 5 blocks and a thruster as their bot.

The third system is to not let people buy their way into battle, the CRF in RC was at first interesting with a hefty fee for a rent system, but it later became a purchase system.
The CRF has caused many issues, such as people going for the prettiest bots which usually end up being hollow and the shittiest bots, as well as prolonging the race for the meta.
