D.R.O.N.E., which i'll now refer to as Drone, was an incredibly styled game.
The environment, buildings, inside and everything perfectly matches each other.
Even the cube building system is just hidden in there, though much building wasn't the case anyways.

Drone itself suffered more from gameplay design issues, a 6DOF game is really difficult to play as enemies can come from anywhere and teamplay becomes a nightmare when identification is barely visible due to the smaller glow colors, free spawning and entirely freedom oriented paint colors.
It also suffers from being difficult in fast paced battles and environments being a hassle rather than a tactic.
Map mastery itself is just entirely void in the custom made maps.

The cube textures and the glow spread perfectly present a futuristic realistic style in the light of all things negative.
