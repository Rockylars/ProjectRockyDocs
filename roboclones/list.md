## Lessons learned from certain games

### Airmen | Multiplayer aircraft battles and missions in cellshaded wooden airships, also has ground vehicles.

- Multiple people on one vessel can work if the game is slow enough and has first person gameplay on the player, it is
  in fact
  very fun.
- Multiple people building one vessel can work if it is big enough and there is high TTK with a lesser need on damage
  routing.

### Constroy | Full low poly futuristic-fantasy vehicle builder with multiplayer promised.

- Multiple cubes per space can solve the problems with smaller cubes feeling underwhelming for a whole CPU and space
  that would otherwise be overpowered if rebalanced.
- Multiple cubes per space gives a great way to build uniquely painted and damage-routed creations.
- Cube shapes should still be limited to a theme no matter how many you can think of.
- Having many cube shapes offers a lot of creativity to the player while still keeping it from being overwhelming.
- (UNTESTED) Doing a 100 CPU range based match system rather than Elo-rating or separated progression tiers might be a
  much
  better solution for horizontal progression.
- (UNTESTED) An inventory solely based on your currency, no inventory but instead parts being bought and sold
  automatically with
  full price upon selling might be a much more pleasant system to work with, also reducing the interface to a fused shop
  and inventory.

### CrossOut

- You don't need flying vehicles to have fun, even just cars and hovers is great (they have since added these).
- Connecting bots together is not that useful, even if properly functional.
- A harpoon to slam enemies around and drag them with you is amazingly fun.
- Explosive parts on your own bot are a fun challenge to work with.
- AI players can fill the gap in the early match time, even if replaced right after with a real player.

### D.R.O.N.E. | Abandoned futuristic multiplayer 6DOF drone team battles in highly customizable maps

- Fake trailers are to be avoided, always present the game's real state.
- 6DOF is not good for fast paced battles especially in teams due to lack of coordination (this can work for an arena
  style),
  claustrophobic movements and being attacked from an impossible to track place as the enemy will have already moved by
  the time
  you try to get to them.
- High details on cubes can work to make the unrealistic shape of the cubes match with the high detail movement parts
  and guns
  to create visual cohesion.
- Map customization leads to a complete loss in strategy due to the lack of map mastery, only respawns can make this
  better,
  but not by much.

### MachineCraft

- Highly customizable shapes and rotations are great for the most custom vehicles you can think of, but the
  entertainment of it
  will drop unless highly invested to the point of full custom animated characters.
- High skill entry is not always a good thing when it comes to retention, as this game has an insanely high amount of
  work behind
  each build.

### Procelio | Not yet finished futuristic/army vehicle builder and battler

- High speed sleds are very fun to use when they smoothly travel the terrain.
- Multi level maps are incredibly fun with tricky ramps and half loops to get from one to the other, as well as all the
  tunnels.
- Huge maps are a bit too much when it comes to a team gameplay map, a free for all would even struggle with the absurd
  size a
  map can be.
- A hub multiplayer mode is very fun for people to just join into whenever they want.
- Screen transitions keep the immersion smooth and seamless.
- Switching to server-side physics for close combat is a good choice with no real input-lag other than the initial
  switch.
- Gathering talented creators who can make beautiful bots for your showcase is a must.
- Making wings too realistic becomes not so fun to play with.
- Yaw on planes is a must have.
- Paint is not always needed, just black and white can be enough.
- Having too many color options can make gradients and color balances annoying, having a tool that plans out supportive
  colors and such
  like those you can find online (https://www.canva.com/colors/color-wheel/) is a need, though you can always just
  google them.
- Right click to repaint aimed at color with held color is much better than RC's repaint everything with held color, we
  actually implemented this in Constroy before they did.

### Robocraft | Multiplayer somewhat futuristic vehicle builder and battler, team focused but has some free for all

- Having 10 tiers in a vertical progression system with 30,000 active concurrent players is still too many, you need 5
  tiers if
  you choose vertical progression in this genre or else it will be too much to grind for and will separate the
  playerbase too much.
- You do not change your core gameplay loop, ever, you can change the design a lot but you do not just switch from
  vertical
  progression to horizontal progression to no progression to random progression to whatever and all the way back around.
- Having a single weapon system will often not suffice, as even subclasses can sometimes have weapons be very limited in
  how
  it can be used, if at all.
- You should not have more than 2-3 weapons/abilities total per build, having 5 is absurd and only leads to having a
  divided
  set of groups that are either focused single/double weapon players, big gun swiss knife players, 1 gun 4 ability
  players,
  total dipshit same weapon different sizes players and everything ever players.
- Random rewards should only come into play when you have a bonus, not the main reward, lootboxes are not a fun way to
  reward the player.
- You should not have bots that are 5 times the size of a normal one, players can not handle this responsiblity, you
  should do
  twice as big at the very most as a gimmick and then also have 0-3 per team, not just one.
- Medics will be selfish, stupid and non-existent, you must encourage people to play medic by rewarding healing the same
  as damage
  and by giving a bonus while also making it a still exciting and enjoyable experience, spotting bonuses can help here
  as well.
- AI players tend to become feeders for kills rather than supportive helpers.
- You should never do an update before a major holiday or weekend as the meta can effectively gridlock your entire game
  into
  annoyance.
- You need a minimum CPU usage (Such as 300 or 500) to enter battle, as well as a gun and movement requirement.
- An unsanitized community bot sharing platform will be 90% trash, 5% pretty bots but trash combat and 5% complete
  perfect meta
  builds. It will only push people to buy the pretty stuff or the big stuff, as well as make the meta spread way faster
  than
  normal.
- A community bot sharing platform will remove all the creative input of experimenting and developing your own build as
  well
  as understanding what works and why, as you could just buy what already works.
- Legs can be a problem when only 2 are required, you must fall over when only 1 is left.
- Center of mass support is a horrible mechanic that leads to flying walls that should never stay upright.
- Instant acceleration removes inertia and makes drones/tsticks become the ultimate vehicle due to their infinite
  control and
  response time.
- You must keep a barrier between veteran and new players or else they will be completely stomped and will not feel like
  playing
  further.
- You can not expect coordination or complicated mechanics in the heat of battle with randoms, even if they're a voice
  chat platoon.
- You need a sense of mass in the bot, if it flies off and goes super smooth, then this somewhat kills immersion even if
  it can
  be more fun to play with. What works best is a proper heaviness to it all, with acceleration in falls and increasing
  particles/sounds/screenshake the heavier you hit the ground. Too much weight can make flying annoying as all hell
  though, a proper
  balance must be struck.
- Getting the full price back when selling parts makes building a lot less stress free.

### Robocraft Infinity

- Bigger hitboxes are not fun to work with, when done as a balancing factor they should not be over the top big.
- Camera control for controller support and "more intuitive" mech leg or tank control is not worth the fucking aim
  assist it
  brings in, as well as the hated movement assist for flying vehicles.
- AI's need to have some intelligence other than shoot on sight.

### Robocraft Royale

- Don't make a battle royale spinoff or gamemode, a free-for-all gamemode is fine though, but will always result in low
  players
  unfair matches, certain parts being useless for that mode, killsteals and teamers.

### Robocrash | Simple cellshaded vehicle builder with damage testing, later on evolved into Airmen.

- Pen testing damage is highly important for a game about damage-routing.

### Rover Rage | Multiplayer on-the-fly vehicle builder, mid-battle reconfiguring and simple mobile controls.

- Building or rebuilding your bot mid battle is exciting and can work, but not with more than 20 armor cubes at the very
  max.
- Rebuilding your bot mid battle requires a map with a lot of large rocks to hide behind and a protective bubble upon
  spawning
  back in.
- Rebuilding your bot is not that helpful in teamplay, a free for all does best with it, one with not that many players
  either
  as well.

### Starship EVO

- Stretching cubes is an interesting way to build things.

### SteamCraft | Dead multiplayer Russian assetflip vehicle builder and battles containing steam-themed air and ground vehicles with futuristic weapons.

- Visual cohesion is extremely important, your game will look horrible without the map, buildings, weapons, music, cubes
  and
  theme working together, the cube shapes already make this a challenge, so it must be taken seriously.

### Trailmakers

- Multiselection and dragging to move parts are an interesting and unique way of assembling a vehicle rather quickly.

### Galactic Junk League

- Visual cohesion, cool narration and a unique visual style are not everything, you actually need a game more
  interesting than a
  slow space shooter as having a game where you can die in a second or live for 20 years in a battle with a same
  playstyle each time
  gets boring rather quickly.
- You need more than just space vehicles to keep some variety in, even a ton of weapons and abilities get boring after a
  while.

### Bad Piggies

- Simple vehicle challenges are exciting, even if complex.

### Things from YouTube

- A crafting system only works if it actually changes how you play, so let's say rail bots drop rail parts upon kill,
  then you
  must build a bot to take those down to grind for those parts instead of general money. This works but it might make
  certain parts
  almost impossible to obtain as nobody is playing that type. It really will just be a "why even add this, it just makes
  it frustrating"
  case. So don't fucking add a crafting system.
