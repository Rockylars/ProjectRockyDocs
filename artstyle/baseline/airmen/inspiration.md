Airmen takes the cube building to a low poly, cell shaded style, focusing more on quick design than an actual theme.
It feels unique, mystical and very nice for how simplistic it is.
It makes its own mechanics deeper by having connection systems that travel through different types of cubes, such as rods, planks and frames.

I like this simple take, it inspired me to make the world more low poly, although their own parts aren't RC style low poly but moreso just a unique vibe with minimal texturing.
I will take it further though, making the terrain actually low poly.
Although I like the world, eventually you start to desire more, it also uses a blend of higher poly and reflective metal parts that add to the mystical vibe but cause an artistic disconnect.
