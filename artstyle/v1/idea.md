Of course, being a small project means the artstyle is usually set as the last priority.
We at first experimented with highly detailed curves on the cubes to mimic the more high detailed takes on the genre, but the more we started working on this the more we realized there was the same disconnect we had felt with RC.
Instead of feeling a disconnect where the cubes are too fantasy for the gritty world, we felt that the cubes were too perfect for the more desolate land we wanted to make.

Since the cubes were only a small start, we will include that here and move onto v2 with the further story.
For weapons, we had no idea yet, for maps, we slowly started going into more low poly stuff, but again, that'll come in v2.
The building bay was this holo chamber, which was later replaced by a space environment.
