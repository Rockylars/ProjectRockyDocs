The artstyle revision pulled it into a colorful, cellshaded and flat textured (no texture) low poly maps and
The first maps we made had a texturing for transition colors, but we kept retrying those only to realize we should simply increase the color spectrum on the map rather than trying to invent new colors by way of textures.
By this point we already made the decision to fully stick to low poly maps, a unique feature that only Airmen did as well.

Most games in this genre focus on a gritty or futuristic look, but to go complete fantasy and low poly perfectly ties everything together.
Nothing realistic or futuristic realistic, with all objects in the map and objects on the bots being low poly fantasy too.

There was a decision made for an outline as no textures means depth isn't really visible and this is part of cellshading as well.
We used a special way of doing them, where we had a lighter color for inside curves and a darker color for outside curves, emphasizing the bot's shape.
