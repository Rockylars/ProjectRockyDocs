Knowing the difference in these systems is rather important.
A lot of weapons can come with many projectile alterations such as homing, instant travel, long distance, short distance, arcing, explosive, piercing, damage direction bias, gravity inducing, volley fire, limited ammo, gravity-less, healing, etc.

The main knowledge is to know these categories, which are the following:
- Primary
  - always functions on its own, it is able to be ran solo without any help and does lots of damage or healing.
  - Situational weapons work poorly as primary weapons but can be designed as primary weapons.
- Pseudo primary
  - primary, but it's only like 75% effective on its own in terms of power, it's on the in between of a secondary and primary.
  - Comparable to a TF2 shotgun or pistol, deadly in a 1v1 but ineffective in crowds.
- Secondary
  - Secondaries are small extras like Tesla blades, flares, smoke guns, missile pods and autoguns.
  - They are situational, small abilities that are either limited in ammo or have limited range.
  - They usually have low power or extremely high power but require you to be super close up.
  - They are the offensive version of a tertiary ability.
- Tertiary
  - Abilities you can use on a cooldown.
  - Often something like teleportation, invisibility, energy boost, speed boost or shielding.

Single weapon systems are highly discouraged, original Robocraft started with it and slightly stepped into having a secondary by having the Tesla blade melee option but mainly stayed just primary as that weapon was often considered a massive clutch or a joke.
Having a single weapon system causes you to not be able to make situational weapons such as anti-air weapons or shotgun weapons, as they would either be too strong or barely useful.
Higher health lower nominal count weapons also fall into this problem, just becoming the age old saying of "it's either god tier or god awful".

There are the following multi weapon systems of the following:
- primary + secondary
  - Original RC came with single primary weapons but with the introduction of tesla was now in this category.
- primary + primary + tertiary
  - Basically modern RC, where you have multiple primaries and a module.
  - Even though it's a multi weapon system where situational weapons can show up, it just ends up making everything too strong.
- primary + secondary + tertiary
  - This is how procelio does it, they have a lot of small and very situational secondary weapons that can still overwhelm you when you do use em in the right situation.
  - It's a great system but comes with balancing problems in the secondaries.
- pseudo primary + pseudo primary + tertiary
  - This is the system we're choosing, it ends up with less powerful weapons but no balancing issues of small and very situational secondaries.
  - It will create primaries that can be extremely situational while allowing less situational ones to exist the same but never be too strong.
